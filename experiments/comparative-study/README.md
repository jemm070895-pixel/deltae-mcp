# C — Comparative empirical study

## Research question
Does using a structured ΔE contract/toolchain, compared with conventional written requirements, measurably affect implementation time, hidden-case correctness, clarification burden, maintenance time, or maintenance regressions?

This is an open empirical question, not a claim that ΔE is better.

## Design
The prepared design is a randomized crossover comparison. The intended cohort is at least 8 independent implementers, preferably 12 or more.

Positive, neutral, and negative results are all informative.

## How to participate
1. Download the public enrollment package above.
2. Do not begin either condition and do not seek hidden evaluator material.
3. Complete `PRE_ASSIGNMENT_FORM.txt` honestly, including prior ΔE/Route A/Route B exposure.
4. Return the completed form through the external-results upload request below, preferably prefixed `C_COMPARATIVE_`.
5. Wait for randomized assignment and first-condition materials.
6. Preserve timing, clarification, frozen-source, first-run, and maintenance evidence exactly as instructed in the assigned protocol.

## Participation
Developers, research software engineers, researchers, and students may volunteer. Participation is voluntary and unpaid.

The study materials must preserve condition separation, randomization, hidden evaluation, and first-run evidence. Therefore evaluator answers and both experimental conditions are not published together in this repository.

## Important separation
Participation in this study is not the same as:
- the individual clean-room implementation exercise; or
- the cross-machine execution exercise.

Evidence is recorded separately. Prior exposure does not have to be hidden; it must be disclosed so it can be handled during assignment/analysis.

## Current stage
Enrollment/pre-assignment is publicly open. Condition materials are released only after assignment so the randomized crossover design and hidden evaluation are not undermined.
